# Skills content review (independent, 2026-10-22)

Findings from a fresh reviewer that read 18 skills in full against the real tool definitions. This is the fix list for the 23 rendered skills.

I have everything I need; no further reads are required. Summary of what the evidence established before the findings:

- `similar_items` returns, per match and per source, only counts (`lines`, `suppliers`, `buyers`), a `price_min`/`price_max` range and `last_date` — never a supplier name, a quantity per order or a dated price series (src/services/analytics/similar_items.py:117-175).
- `invoice_reconciliation` requires `supplier`, starts from invoice lines, and returns lines, differences, `receipts_by_po` (counts only), `pos_without_receipt` and findings — no invoice status, hold reason, credit note, or PO-with-no-invoice (src/services/invoice_reconciliation.py:101-219).
- `obligation_tracker` returns an expiry/auto-renew calendar plus notice/termination/renewal/review clauses — no payment schedule (src/services/contract_tools/tracker.py:77-146). `build_spend_cube` dimensions are supplier, month, currency, country, region, buyer, payment_terms, status — no category, item, cost centre or project.
- Under `AGENT_SKILL_TOOL_SCOPE` the agent is offered only tools named with a `tool:` prefix (src/orchestration/context_builder.py:45), so a tool a skill needs but does not cite is unavailable to it.
- Router checks below were run with the real deterministic router (`src/orchestration/skill_router.py`) against this repo's `skills/` folder; no model, no file writes.

Line numbers refer to the file's own numbering (as `cat -n` shows).

## 1. Tool misuse (13 findings)

1. **buying-channel-guidance/SKILL.md:25** — "Use `tool:similar_items` for suppliers we already buy the item from and the prices paid". `similar_items` returns supplier *counts*, never names; `supplier_discovery` (which does name them) is not cited, so it is not even offered. Quality bar :65 "Any supplier named is on our records", worked example :75 ("a hardware reseller… the price paid last quarter") and scenario m3 ("Who is our supplier for lab consumables?" → "From contracts and items on record") are unachievable.
2. **tail-spend-management/SKILL.md:25, :39** — "Use `tool:similar_items` for whether a tail supplier's items are already bought from a contracted or larger supplier" / step 3 "look for the same items on record from a contracted supplier (similar items plus contracts). A match is a consolidation candidate". No cited tool lists what a tail supplier sells (the cube is by supplier with no items) and `similar_items` cannot say *which* other supplier sells it. The core consolidation step cannot be executed; scenario m3 "Which tail suppliers could move to Acme? … via similar items" cannot be met. Same error in **supplier-segmentation/SKILL.md:28, :39** ("an item with no alternative supplier on record (similar items)"), **policy-exception-request/SKILL.md:29** (and scenario c1 "Alternatives shown with dates"), and **supply-continuity-planning/SKILL.md:25** (less severe: it also cites `supplier_discovery`).
3. **budget-commitment-tracking/SKILL.md:23-26** — four of five data tools cannot deliver what they are asked for: :24 "`tool:invoice_reconciliation` for open orders and their invoiced versus uninvoiced value for the scope" (needs a supplier; starts from invoices, so a PO with no invoice — the open commitment — is not listed); :26 "`tool:obligation_tracker` for scheduled payments and milestones" (no payment data); :25 "`tool:contract_pipeline` for the contracted commitments ahead in the period" (returns contracts ending in a horizon with whole-contract value, not amounts due); :23 "build_spend_cube … by the dimension that matches the scope" when :20 lists budget, category, cost centre, project — none is a cube dimension. Worked example :76 asserts "the maintenance contract's remaining scheduled payments from its tracker". Scenarios t2, t4, e3 rest on these.
4. **procurement-reporting/SKILL.md:24** "`tool:build_spend_cube` for spend by supplier and category" (the tool states "There is no category"); :39 "Spend: total, by category"; :27 "`tool:invoice_reconciliation` for invoices without orders and open findings in the period" (supplier-scoped, cannot give a period-wide figure); :40 "share of spend under a live agreement" (no tool computes coverage). Scenarios e2/e3 expect category-level answers.
5. **framework-call-off/SKILL.md:23, :26** — "`tool:search_contracts` for the frameworks… and their lots" and "`tool:supplier_lookup` for the suppliers on the lot" — the register holds no lots and `supplier_lookup` takes one name. Scenario t3 ("Which suppliers are on lot 3…?" → "from the records"), quality bar :68 and scenario c2 ("The lot list on the register…") presume a field that does not exist.
6. **ordering-and-inventory/SKILL.md:24** "`tool:similar_items` for the item's order and price history on record, quantities per order and the suppliers" — none of those are returned; :25 "`tool:invoice_reconciliation` for the orders and deliveries… as the demand history" (supplier required; receipts carry no quantities); :28 "`critic.annualised_rate` for an annual rate from a shorter period" — that formula is the compound annual rate of change between an anchor *price* and a current *price* over N years, not an annualised quantity. Scenario t1 "Demand from two years of orders… via the registry" is not reachable.
7. **rebates-and-incentives/SKILL.md:28** "`critic.annualised_rate` to project the period's rate" — same misuse (the right call is `deal.scaled_total`, used correctly by budget-commitment-tracking). Scenario e1 "Qualifying spend recounted net using reconciliation" — reconciliation has no credit-note data and the cube is gross; netting is not achievable with the cited tools.
8. **sla-design/SKILL.md:27** and **supplier-development-plan/SKILL.md:29** — "`otif.delivery_performance` where a delivery measure is proposed, so the measurement is the platform's definition". The formula needs per-delivery rows (promised_date, received_date, ordered_qty, received_qty) plus governed tolerances; no cited tool returns delivery rows and the formula's own note says the corpus holds no promised dates or receipt quantities. ## 2. Procurement-practice errors / internal contradictions (6)

1. **delivery-issue-handling/config.yaml:3** `rejection_notice_days: 5 # … when the contract states no window`, applied at SKILL.md:40 and expected by scenario m2 ("otherwise the configured default labelled as a default"). A rejection window is a legal deadline; where the contract is silent the buyer has a "reasonable time", not a number a skill chose. This contradicts the skill's own quality bar :67-68 ("none from general practice"; "Deadlines are computed only from a stated window"). The expected behaviour in m2 is wrong: it should escalate to legal, not supply five days.
2. **buying-channel-guidance/SKILL.md:39** and config `quote_band_gbp: 5000` / `tender_band_gbp: 50000` — the skill tells requesters "below the configured quote band, requisition with one quote … above the configured tender band, a sourcing event" from config defaults. Elsewhere the library states `approval_threshold_policy` is the only purchasing-authority policy and `check_policy` says limits "come from the policy, never from you". A buyer being told "one quote is enough under 5,000" on a skill default is a compliance exposure.
3. **policy-exception-request/SKILL.md:40** lists exception grounds from memory ("genuine urgency, sole capable supplier, compatibility, value below a band, and whatever else the policy lists") while quality bar :68 says grounds "are quoted, never paraphrased from memory"; "value below a band" is not an exception ground at all (below a band the rule does not apply).
4. **procurement-reporting/SKILL.md:41** "by status exactly as the ledger states (claimed, validated, realised)" and scenario t4 "validated and realised separated from claimed" — `savings_tracker` reports saved (avoided + recovered + realised) and claimed; "validated" is not a ledger status, so the report would invent a category.
5. **contract-amendment-review/SKILL.md:38** "If none is stored, say the procedure is unknown and the amendment should state its own authority" — an amendment cannot confer authority on itself; the fallback is the contract's variation clause or legal advice. Also scenario t4's expect "obligation tracker updated in the report, not in the record" describes a non-existent operation.
6. **dispute-and-claims/SKILL.md:42, :56** "A claim above `legal_referral_gbp` goes to legal" / "legal owns the claim above the configured referral value" vs :16 "legal owns the claim" unconditionally. The threshold implies claims under £10k may be run without legal, which the rest of the skill denies.

## Verdict

The 23 rendered skills are not of the same quality as the hand-written ones. Structurally they are identical, but their tool lines were written from the tool's *name* rather than its parameters and outputs: `similar_items` is used as a supplier finder in six skills, `invoice_reconciliation` as a scope-wide ledger, `obligation_tracker` as a payment schedule, the spend cube "by category" in four, and `critic.annualised_rate` as a run-rate projector — errors that the hand-written skills explicitly guard against in prose ("it takes an item, never a supplier name"; "Invoices carry no category"; named parameters like `containing`, `cross_border`, `lifetime_items`). The rendered scenarios reuse a template of either/or expects and expect data the tools cannot return, whereas urgent-sourcing, total-cost-of-ownership and supplier-offboarding pin one behaviour per scenario and name the tool and calculation that proves it. The procurement content of the rendered set is mostly sound (Kraljic, rebate basis, change control, dispute ladders), with two practice-level faults (a skill-invented rejection deadline; config-default quote/tender bands presented to requesters). Finding counts: tool misuse 13, practice/contradiction 6, scenario quality 12, routing 8, leak/write 3 — 42 in total, of which 36 fall in the rendered skills.
