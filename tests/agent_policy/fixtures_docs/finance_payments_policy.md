# Finance: Payments and Refunds Policy

Business area: Finance. Owner: Finance Manager.

1. Refunds and credits

1.1 Refunds or credits above $500 need approval from the Finance Manager. Refunds above $10,000 are not allowed.

1.2 The agent must get approval from the Finance Manager before it runs email dispatch (the run_email_dispatch tool) to send payment emails to suppliers.

2. Good practice

2.1 Finance staff should review the refunds of the previous month at each month end.
